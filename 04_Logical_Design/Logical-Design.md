## 1. Input
  Input refers to the information entered into a computer system by the user.
  In the School Attendance Management System, the input includes the student's ID, full name, class, attendance date and attendance status. 
  The teacher or administrator enters these details into the system so that student information and attendance can be recorded correctly.
## 2. Processing
  Processing refers to the operations performed by the computer on the information entered by the user.
  In the School Attendance Management System, processing includes checking student details, verifying student IDs, recording attendance, searching for student records and calculating the attendance percentage.
  These operations help the system manage student attendance accurately.
  ## 3. Output
Output refers to the information produced by the computer after processing the data.
In the School Attendance Management System, the output includes student details, registration confirmation messages, attendance records and attendance reports.
The system also displays the number of days a student was present or absent and the attendance percentage.
